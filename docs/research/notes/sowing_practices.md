## 1. Winter wheat (bread and durum) and barley in rainfed Central Anatolia: date, seed rate, depth, row spacing, and adjustments

### Takeaway
Research cited by TİGEM puts the best rainfed sowing window in Central Anatolia and Konya at **15 Sep–10 Oct for wheat** and **21 Sep–19 Oct for barley**. Seed rate is best set in **viable seeds/m²** rather than kg/da:
- wheat: 500–600 seeds/m²
- winter barley: 450–550 seeds/m²
- late spring barley: 500–600 seeds/m²

The seed count is converted to kg/da with a thousand-kernel-weight (TKW) formula. Use the lower end with a good seedbed and on-time sowing, and the upper end with a poor seedbed or late sowing. Depth is **4–6 cm** (3–4 cm for spring barley), and **at least 5–6 cm** when the soil is dry. Row spacing in regional trials is **20 cm**. I found no official regional source that states a row-spacing recommendation explicitly.

### Cited Findings

**Sowing date**
- In Central Anatolia and the Konya area, research found the best sowing time to be **15 Sep–10 Oct for wheat** and **21 Sep–19 Oct for barley** (Keklikçi et al., 1991; Topal, 1997). Coastal regions sow later, 15 Nov–15 Dec — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- TİGEM-B4 Table 8 lists the optimum wheat sowing date by area — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)

  | Orta Anadolu | "within October" | Anon. 1977 |
  | Orta Anadolu | "first week of October" | Doğan & Küçükçakar 1987 |
  | Konya, rainfed (kuruda) | 15 Sep–10 Oct | Keklikçi et al. 1991 |
  | Konya, irrigated (suluda) | 1 Oct–10 Oct | Yılmaz et al. 1993 |
  | Muş–Van (cold east) | 15 Sep–15 Oct | — |
  | Erzurum–Kars | 15 Aug–1 Sep | — |
  | Erzurum | 22 Aug–3 Sep | — |
  | Edirne | mid-October | — |
  | Tekirdağ | 21 Oct–11 Nov | — |

- Barley: Gerek (1963) found **15 October** to be the best barley sowing date in Central Anatolia, and that "autumn sowing yields at least twice spring sowing" — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- TİGEM farm practice in Central Anatolia: sow **15 Sep–15 Oct**, wheat first and then barley. The reason given is that this period averages only 20–25 mm of rain, so sowing in it avoids the autumn-drought risk — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- TİGEM Table 7 gives 1996–2000 mean rainfall at its Central Anatolian farms. September 6–12 mm, October 29–49 mm. Examples [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf):

  | Farm | September (mm) | October (mm) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Altınova | 11 | 38 |
  | Polatlı | 11 | 34 |
  | Konuklar | 8 | 49 |
  | Gözlü | 8 | 48 |
  | Koçaş | 6 | 45 |
  | Bala | 12 | 29 |
  | Çiçekdağı | 12 | 33 |
  | Malya | 8 | 35 |

- Temperature criterion: the best winter-cereal sowing time is when **seedbed soil temperature is 5–8 °C** — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf). A Bolu İl Tarım leaflet instead says **8–10 °C**. The same leaflet gives Eylül–Kasım for İç Anadolu and says sowing can stretch from early October to end of December — [Bolu İl Tarım [SNIPPET]](https://bolu.tarimorman.gov.tr/Belgeler/agroekoloji/BU%C4%9EDAY%20YET%C4%B0%C5%9eT%C4%B0R%C4%B0C%C4%B0L%C4%B0%C4%9e%C4%B0%20YEN%C4%B0.pdf). The sources disagree: 5–8 °C vs 8–10 °C. The Bolu URL returned 404 or HTML when I fetched it, so it is unverified.
- Target growth stage at winter onset: **1–3 tillers (3–4 leaves)**. Winter hardiness is highest then. Plants at 1–2 leaves, or plants with many tillers, suffer more winter damage — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Risk of sowing too early on fallow:
  - excess autumn top growth, weak seminal roots, and moisture used up early (Kolp et al. 1973)
  - more Hessian fly, take-all, mildew, Septoria, rust and virus
  - late sowing instead raises Rhizoctonia, Typhula and Tilletia (bunt)
  - [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Do not sow spring wheat in Central Anatolia unless forced: "zorunlu kalınmadıkça yazlık ekim yapılmaması tavsiye edilmektedir". In a Central Anatolian barley study, spring sowing lost up to **83%** of yield compared with winter sowing (Gemalmaz 1997). Where annual rainfall is below 600 mm, winter wheat yields at least double spring wheat — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Late sowing in a Konya direct-drilling trial: in 2012, conventional plots could only be made ready by **12 Nov 2012**. The authors note that in the region, sowing after October lowers yields (Topal 1993) — [Gültekin et al. 2017 [PAGE]](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/390551)

**Seed rate**
- Semi-arid areas (300–400 mm) with a well-prepared seedbed — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf):
  - bread wheat **18–19 kg/da**
  - durum **20–21 kg/da**
  - barley **20–22 kg/da**
  - Add **3–5 kg/da** if the seedbed is poorly prepared, the seed is broadcast (serpme), or seed quality is low.
- Technical staff have recommended **18–22 kg/da** for years, while farmers use up to **30 kg/da**. TİGEM attributes the gap mainly to seedling death from fertilizer placed with the seed — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- TİGEM Table 10 gives viable seeds/m² for seed screened over a 2–2.2 mm sieve and treated — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf):

  | Crop / type | Viable seeds/m² | Depth (cm) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Bread wheat, high-tillering (Golia, Gerek-79) | 500–550 | 4–6 |
  | Bread wheat, normal or low-tillering (Bezostaja, Panda) | 550–600 | 4–6 |
  | Durum wheat | 550–600 | 4–6 |
  | Winter (autumn-sown) barley | 450–550 | 4–6 |
  | Late spring-sown barley | 500–600 | 3–4 |

- Adjustment rule (rainfed) — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf):
  - good seedbed and on-time sowing: use the **lower** limit
  - poor seedbed or **late sowing**: use the **upper** limit
  - normal seedbed: use the **mean**
  - irrigated: early sowing takes the lower limit, late sowing the upper.
- Formula — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf):
  - **Seed rate (kg/da) = seeds/m² × TKW (g) × 10 ÷ (purity % × biological value %)**
  - TKW: weigh 4 × 100 seeds, average them, multiply by 10.
  - Purity: remove foreign matter from a 100 g sample.
  - Which biological value to use:
    - germination capacity (8-day %): plump, current-year seed sown on time
    - germination speed (4-day %): plump seed sown off-time
    - emergence capacity (12-day %, under 3 cm of sand): old or shrivelled seed sown on time
    - emergence speed (7-day %): old or shrivelled seed sown off-time
  - Worked example for Gerek-79: 525 seeds/m², TKW 38 g, germination 95%, purity 98%. The document states the result as "20 kg/da".
  - **Caution:** the arithmetic 525×38×10/(95×98) gives **21.4 kg/da**, not 20. The document's example is rounded or wrong, so the site should compute the value rather than copy "20".
- Konya: **500 seeds/m²** (Mülayim & Topal 1991) and **18–20 kg/da** wheat (Yılmaz et al. 1993) — [TİGEM-B4 Table 9 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Other Central Anatolia optima in Table 9 (seeds/m²) — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf). These older trials range much lower than the modern 500–600:

- Older research found **300–350** viable seeds/m² sufficient. Recent research recommends **500–700**, using varieties with many fertile tillers rather than high tillering. Ankara Univ. trials found the top yield at **500–600** seeds/m² for both durum and bread wheat — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Haymana trial practice: wheat drilled at **550–600 seeds/m²** and winter lentil at **300–350 seeds/m²** — [Adak 1999 [PAGE]](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/118589)
- Other leaflets (not Central Anatolia; listed to show disagreement):
  - Bolu: "20–25 kg/da generally" — [Bolu [SNIPPET]](https://bolu.tarimorman.gov.tr/Belgeler/agroekoloji/BU%C4%9EDAY%20YET%C4%B0%C5%9eT%C4%B0R%C4%B0C%C4%B0L%C4%B0%C4%9e%C4%B0%20YEN%C4%B0.pdf)
  - Gaziantep: 15–18 kg/da drilled, and increase "3-3 kg" for weak seed, low germination or late sowing (probably a typo) — [Gaziantep İl Tarım [SNIPPET]](https://gaziantep.tarimorman.gov.tr/Belgeler/kutumenu/bro%C5%9F%C3%BCr/Tarla%20Bitkileri/bugday.pdf)
  - Adana: 22–24 kg/da drilled, 26–28 kg/da broadcast — [Adana İl Tarım [SNIPPET]](https://adana.tarimorman.gov.tr/Belgeler/SUBELER/bitkisel_uretim_ve_bitki_sagligi_sube_mudurlugu/hububat_yetistiriciligi_ve_mucadelesi/Bu%C4%9Fday%20Yeti%C5%9Ftiricili%C4%9Fi.pdf)
  - Trakya TAE: target **500 viable seeds/m²**; 16–18 kg/da for small-seeded, 18–20 for high-tillering, 20–22 for large-seeded varieties — [TTAE [PAGE]](https://arastirma.tarimorman.gov.tr/ttae/Sayfalar/Detay.aspx?SayfaId=69)

**Sowing depth**
- Winter wheat: **4–6 cm** of soil cover is optimal [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf):
  - deeper than 6 cm is dangerous for small-seeded varieties
  - deeper than 8 cm, seedlings may fail to emerge, or emerge very weak
  - semi-dwarf and dwarf varieties have short coleoptiles, so they need extra care with depth
- **Dry soil:** when soil moisture is insufficient, seed must be covered by **at least 5–6 cm** of soil. Otherwise light rains that wet only the surface start germination, and a dry spell then kills the seedlings ("Ala Tav") — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- **Late sowing:** sow at "normal" depth, not deep, so seedlings emerge quickly and make up for the lost time — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Coastal areas with enough moisture and TKW above 20 g: **2–4 cm** — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Barley in Turkey: **winter 4–6 cm, spring 3–4 cm** — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Trakya TAE: 4–5 cm for small seed, 5–6 cm for large seed — [TTAE [PAGE]](https://arastirma.tarimorman.gov.tr/ttae/Sayfalar/Detay.aspx?SayfaId=69). Bolu: 4–6 cm depending on soil moisture — [Bolu [SNIPPET]](https://bolu.tarimorman.gov.tr/Belgeler/agroekoloji/BU%C4%9EDAY%20YET%C4%B0%C5%9eT%C4%B0R%C4%B0C%C4%B0L%C4%B0%C4%9e%C4%B0%20YEN%C4%B0.pdf)
- Root depth: winter wheat roots in Central Anatolia can exceed **1.5 m**; barley reaches **80–90 cm** — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)

**Row spacing and direction**
- Regional trials use **20 cm**. Example: Taner 2022, a Bahri Dağdaş Bitkisel Araştırma Dergisi trial in Central Anatolia with 14 bread-wheat varieties, rainfed, **550 seeds/m²** — [Dergipark 2555787 [SNIPPET]](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/2555787). This is a trial layout, not an official recommendation.
- An Ankara Univ. lecture note says cereal drills are usually set at **15–20 cm** — [Ankara Üniv. açıkders [SNIPPET]](https://acikders.ankara.edu.tr/mod/resource/view.php?id=151309)
- **East–west rows** gave more spikes/m² and grains/spike than north–south rows. TİGEM recommends east–west where practical — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)

**Dry soil and waiting for rain**
- In rainfed areas the sowing date is set by catching suitable moisture after rain, so it varies a lot between years — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- If sowing into a **dry seedbed** with a drill that puts fertilizer and seed in the same band, TİGEM suggests (citing Sade 2001) spreading the fertilizer on the surface and mixing it in before sowing. The aim is to avoid fertilizer drawing water from the seed, raising osmotic pressure and causing ammonia toxicity. TİGEM accepts the downsides of this (N loss, P/K fixation) — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- News reports describe dry autumns pushing sowing into November in Yozgat, and seed rate rising when sowing is delayed in Konya — [CNN Türk Yozgat [SNIPPET]](https://www.cnnturk.com/yerel-haberler/yozgat/yozgatta-kuraklik-nedeniyle-bugday-ekimi-kasim-ayina-sarkti-2360959), [Dergipark 118589 search summary [SNIPPET]](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/118589)

**Soil texture**
- Root growth is shallow in compact soils with little pore volume and deep in loose, well-aerated soils. TİGEM stresses deepening soil "liveliness" in dry-farming areas — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- I found no numeric texture rule for cereal depth. The legume booklet has one for bean (fasulye), not cereals, which may serve as an analogy:
  - **heavy clay 4–6 cm**
  - **light sandy 6–8 cm**
  - **7–8 cm** where pre-sowing herbicide is used
  - [GKGM-YB [PAGE]](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/GKGM/Belgeler/Uretici_Bilgi_Kosesi/Dokumanlar/yemeklik_baklagil.pdf)

### Inferences
- A site rule set that follows TİGEM-B4:
  - Window: wheat 15 Sep–10 Oct, barley 21 Sep–19 Oct for Konya/Central Anatolia plains. Cold districts should lean early and warmer districts later; this is not quantified for Sivas or Yozgat.
  - Seeds/m²: 500–600 (wheat), 450–550 (winter barley). Take the low end when the seedbed is good and sowing is on time, the high end when sowing is late or the seedbed is poor.
  - Convert to kg/da with the TKW formula and the user's TKW, purity and germination values.
  - Depth: 4–6 cm, and at least 5–6 cm if the soil is dry; normal (not deep) if late.
- "Late sowing → increase seed rate by X%" is **not** stated as a percentage in any official source I found. The defensible rule is "move to the upper end of the seeds/m² range", which is about +10–20% across 500 → 600. That figure is my arithmetic, not a source value. A second rule from the same source: "+3–5 kg/da for a poor seedbed, broadcast sowing or poor seed".

### Gaps
- I found no official il-level sowing windows for Ankara, Eskişehir, Kayseri, Sivas-Gemerek or Yozgat-Boğazlıyan. The il müdürlüğü pages did not surface, and the Bolu PDF returned 404 or HTML.
- I could not open a Bahri Dağdaş or TARM official "buğday yetiştiriciliği" guide page with numbers.
- I found no explicit regional row-spacing recommendation, only the 20 cm trial practice.
- I found no quantitative rule for sandy vs clay soils for cereals.

## 2. Chickpea (nohut) and lentil (mercimek) in Central Anatolia

### Takeaway
The official booklet recommends:
- **Lentil:** **winter sowing in October** for Central Anatolia and the Geçit regions. Seed rate is 14–15 kg/da (large green, winter) or 8–10 kg/da (small, winter), **+1–2 kg/da for winter-kill risk**. Depth 4–5 cm. Rows 15–20 cm, or **30 cm where rainfall is under 350 mm**.
- **Chickpea:** **early spring, at the first date the field can be entered**, about 7–10 days before the last spring frost. Target **100 plants/m²** in Central Anatolia. Seed 8–10 kg/da drilled. Rows 45 cm (machine hoeing) or 30–35 cm (hand weeding). Depth 5–6 cm.

Winter chickpea outyields spring chickpea at high elevation in Haymana trials, but Ascochyta and weeds limit it. Inoculation is required where chickpea is grown for the first time.

### Cited Findings

**Lentil** (all [GKGM-YB [PAGE]](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/GKGM/Belgeler/Uretici_Bilgi_Kosesi/Dokumanlar/yemeklik_baklagil.pdf) unless noted)
- Sowing time: winter lentil is sown in **October in Orta Anadolu and the Geçit regions**, and in **November in South-East Anatolia**. Spring lentil should be sown in **late February–early March**. Delay causes yield loss.
- Lentil ranks first among grain legumes for cold tolerance, so winter sowing is recommended. Germination needs at least 15 °C and is best at 18–25 °C.
- Seed rate, drilled:

  | Lentil type | Winter (kg/da) | Spring (kg/da) |
  |---|---|---|
  | Large green (yeşil pul, Sultani) | 14–15 | 10–12 |
  | Small-seeded | 8–10 | 7–8 |

  - Broadcast: add **at least 2 kg/da**.
  - Winter lentil in Central Anatolia and Geçit: add **1–2 kg/da** to allow for winter damage.
- Row spacing: **15–20 cm** gave better yields. Where rainfall is **under 350 mm**, **30 cm** outyielded narrower rows.
- Depth: **4–5 cm** for rapid emergence.
- Method: the best method is row sowing with a cereal drill. Broadcasting is common on slopes.
- Seedbed, two routes:
  - (a) Plough stubble **15–20 cm** right after cereal harvest while the soil still has light moisture ("gölge tav"), then wait. After the first autumn rains, once weeds have germinated, do a second pass with cultivator + harrow or goble disk + harrow.
  - (b) Leave the stubble untouched until the first autumn rains, plough **15–20 cm** when the soil is tav, then do a second pass.
- Fertilizer: about **12–14 kg/da DAP** at sowing.
- Weeds: the main yield limiter in winter lentil. For small spring-lentil fields in Central Anatolia, **two hand weedings** are recommended. Some post-emergence graminicides can be used at the **2–4 leaf** stage. Pre-sowing soil herbicides can reduce emergence.
- Haymana trial practice: winter lentil drilled at **300–350 seeds/m²** — [Adak 1999 [PAGE]](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/118589)

**Chickpea** (all [GKGM-YB [PAGE]](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/GKGM/Belgeler/Uretici_Bilgi_Kosesi/Dokumanlar/yemeklik_baklagil.pdf) unless noted)
- Sowing time:
  - Nationally, mid-February to April depending on region; sow **7–10 days before the last spring frosts**.
  - With **Ascochyta-resistant varieties** now available, sowing in Central Anatolia is recommended "at the first date the field can be entered in spring" ("ilkbaharda tarlaya girilebilen ilk tarihlerde").
  - In mild-winter areas, earlier sowing raises yield.
- Density: optimum **100 plants/m² in Orta Anadolu**, compared with 40 in South Marmara and 50–75 in the Aegean.
- Row spacing: **45 cm** if weeding is by hoe machine, **30–35 cm** if by hand.
- Depth: **5–6 cm**. Deep sowing (10 cm) delays flowering and cuts flower and pod number.
- Seed rate for well-branching koçbaşı (large) types: **10–12 kg/da broadcast**, **8–10 kg/da drilled**.
- Seed quality: germination of **85–95%**. Seed must come from a **disease-free field**, because antraknoz is seed-borne.
- Seedbed:
  - After cereal harvest and before autumn rains, plough with a soklu pulluk **15–20 cm** and leave it until spring.
  - In spring (**first week of March**), as soon as the soil is tav, work it **10–15 cm** with cultivator + harrow or diskaro + harrow.
  - In water-limited areas, avoid unnecessary spring tillage that loses moisture.
  - Note: GKGM-YB also says chickpea germination needs field moisture below 15% of field capacity. This wording looks garbled in the source and should not be used.
- Fertilizer: **2–4 kg/da pure N** before sowing (starter N until nodules work) and **6–8 kg/da P2O5**. DAP (18-46) covers both.
- Inoculation: "where chickpea is grown for the first time, seed must be inoculated with the bacteria" ("ilk defa nohut tarımı yapılacak yerlerde … aşılanması gerekmektedir").
- Weeds:
  - After late spring sowing, weeds are generally not a problem, but continued rains can cause weediness. Small farms can hand-weed once.
  - **Early sowing of resistant varieties gives large yield gains but more weeds**, so weed control is mandatory with early sowing.
- Ascochyta (Ascochyta rabiei): epidemics occur in high-rainfall years and regions, irrigated fields and humid closed valleys. The control listed is resistant or tolerant varieties and clean seed.
- Irrigation note (Konya plain): with antraknoz-tolerant varieties, 3 irrigations raised yield significantly, at flowering start, pod set and grain fill. If only one irrigation is possible, give it at flowering start.

**Winter chickpea evidence in Central Anatolia**
- **Haymana, 1994–1996**, 11 genotypes, winter-sown at high elevation: winter sowing gave higher yields than spring because of a better water regime. Top yield was **357 kg/da** (line AkN 454). **Ascochyta and weeds** were the main limiting factors — [Acarindex abstract, "Yüksek Alanlarda Kışlık Nohut Yetiştirme İmkanlarının Araştırılması" [PAGE: abstract only]](https://www.acarindex.com/biotech-studies/yuksek-alanlarda-kislik-nohut-cicer-arietinum-l-yetistirme-imkanlarinin-arastirilmasi-1230423)
- Elsewhere in Turkey, sowing is delayed to spring as protection against antraknoz. Winter sowings yield more but suffer antraknoz and weeds. Diyarbakır trials found high antraknoz in winter sowings — [search summary of Dergipark 346111 / 321938 [SNIPPET]](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/346111). These are not Central Anatolia.
- Official winter-chickpea registration (tescil) reports exist for 2019 and 2026. The trial locations appear to be mainly SE Anatolia and Çukurova — [2026 Kışlık Ekim Nohut Tescil Raporu [SNIPPET]](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/BUGEM/TTSM/Belgeler/Duyuru%20Belgeleri/2026%20Duyuru%20Belgeleri/2026%20Nisan%20Toplant%C4%B1lar/Tescil%20raporlar%C4%B1/2026%20Yemeklik%20Tane%20Baklagiller%20Tescil%20Raporlar%C4%B1/2026%20K%C4%B1%C5%9Fl%C4%B1k%20Ekim%20Nohut%20Tescil%20Raporu.pdf)

**Inoculation evidence** (all [SNIPPET])
- Chickpea, 2001 (Hatay and Sakarya): Rhizobium raised yield **about 20%** over the control — [Dergipark zfdergi 69449 [SNIPPET]](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/zfdergi/article/69449)
- Lentil: inoculation with 5-day-old culture was equivalent to about **5 kg/da mineral N** — [YÖK Açık Bilim 50358 [SNIPPET]](https://acikbilim.yok.gov.tr/handle/20.500.12812/50358)
- Kayseri-Talas (Central Anatolia), 2023: Azkan chickpea with Rhizobium ciceri plus zinc gave the highest yield — [search summary [SNIPPET]](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/4424870)

### Inferences
- For a decision site in Central Anatolia: lentil can be treated as a **winter (October)** crop. Chickpea's default is **early spring**, with "winter chickpea" as an option only for resistant varieties, carrying an explicit Ascochyta and weed-pressure warning.
- The 30 cm row rule for lentil under 350 mm fits Konya, where mean annual rain is 318 mm (Gültekin et al. 2017 [PAGE](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/390551)).

### Gaps
- I found no official step-by-step inoculation procedure for Central Anatolia (dose per kg of seed, mixing in shade, time limit before sowing).
- I found no official winter-chickpea sowing date for Central Anatolia (for example "late Oct–Nov").
- I found no lentil inoculation statement in GKGM-YB. Only chickpea inoculation is stated there.
- I found no herbicide timing beyond the lentil 2–4 leaf note and the "control weeds if sown early" note for chickpea.

## 3. Sowing methods: conventional plough plus drill, combined drill (seed plus fertilizer band), and direct drilling into stubble; seedbed after fallow

### Takeaway
In Konya trials, **placing fertilizer in a separate band** from the seed raised wheat yield 14–23% (Sade et al. 1995) and raised emergence and yield by about 46–68% in a dry autumn (Marakoğlu 2000). The combined drill used put seed at 5 cm and fertilizer 7 cm to the side at 8 cm depth. TİGEM therefore advises avoiding drills that drop seed and fertilizer in the same spot in drought-prone Central Anatolia. Direct drilling is viable in Konya: over 2012–2014 the abstract reports 241 kg/da for direct drilling against 226 kg/da for conventional. Results depend strongly on transition years, sowing date and weeds.

### Cited Findings
- Drills common in Central Anatolia are either single-box (seed and fertilizer mixed) or two-box that still drop both in the same band. In dry soil the fertilizer draws water from the seed, raises osmotic pressure and is toxic (NH3) to seedlings — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- **Sade et al. (1995)**, Konya rainfed, Gerek-79, 1992–94. Two-year mean yields — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf):

  | Method | 2-year mean (kg/da) |
  |---|---|
  | Seed and fertilizer in separate bands | 303 |
  | Fertilizer broadcast and incorporated before sowing | 291 |
  | Mixed with seed, or combined drill with same placement | 289 |

  Separate bands gave **+14–23%** over mixed placement, and the difference was larger in dry years.
- **Marakoğlu (2000)**, Konya, 1999–2000. The separate-band combined drill (seed 5 cm deep, fertilizer **7 cm to the side, 8 cm deep**) was compared with two drills — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf):

  | Compared with | Emergence gain | Yield gain |
  |---|---|---|
  | Seed and fertilizer together at same depth | +49.0% | +67.9% |
  | Separate tubes, same band | +45.9% | +63.9% |

  The autumn was very dry that year.
- Modern TİGEM practice uses pneumatic cereal drills — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf). Trakya TAE recommends combined drills with disc or chisel openers and easily adjusted seed rate — [TTAE [PAGE]](https://arastirma.tarimorman.gov.tr/ttae/Sayfalar/Detay.aspx?SayfaId=69)
- **Direct drilling (DE), Bahri Dağdaş UTAE, Konya rainfed, 2011–2014.** Karahan-99 wheat after fallow, lentil, chickpea, Hungarian vetch and safflower — [Gültekin et al. 2017 [PAGE]](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/390551)
  - Mean annual rain 318 mm, of which 90% falls in the wheat season. Soil is clay, high-lime alluvium.
  - Methods compared:
    - DE: a special drill opens the surface minimally and places seed plus fertilizer with no prior tillage
    - conventional (GE): soklu pulluk, then cultivator + harrow, then spring-tine harrow, then a standard drill
  - Fertilizer for all plots: **7 kg/da P2O5 + 2.4 kg/da N at sowing**, with N topped up to **7 kg/da** in early spring.
  - DE plots used a total herbicide before sowing, and in spring on the DE fallow.
  - Abstract means: GE **226**, DE **241 kg/da**.
  - By previous crop: fallow 286 > lentil 249 > chickpea 228 > vetch 223 > safflower 182 kg/da.
  - Year results:

    | Season | GE (kg/da) | DE (kg/da) | Note |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | 2011–12 | 234 | 204 | transition effect |
    | 2012–13 | 271 | 218 | Both sown the same late date, because GE plots were only ready on 12 Nov 2012. DE soil is cooler, so emergence was weaker. |
    | 2013–14 | — | DE better | Sown at proper dates |

  - Highest single treatment: **DE after lentil, 336 kg/da** (2012–13).
  - The authors stress that sowing date and weed density strongly affect DE results, and that DE needs a good drill plus a rotation.
- Region-wide, DE yields were equal to or better than conventional, and more economical (Gültekin 2012) — [Gültekin et al. 2017 [PAGE]](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/390551)
- Konya chickpea–wheat (Kaya et al. 2010, 2004–07): direct drilling was more profitable for wheat, conventional better for chickpea — [search summary [SNIPPET]](https://dergipark.org.tr/download/article-file/118979)
- Yozgat İl Tarım ran a no-till demonstration with harvest at Saraykent — [Yozgat İl Tarım [SNIPPET]](https://yozgat.tarimorman.gov.tr/Haber/408/Saraykent-Merkez-Toprak-Islemesiz-Tarim-Demonstrasyonu-Hasadi)
- **Seedbed after fallow (Haymana, 1995–98)** — [Adak 1999 [PAGE]](https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/download/article-file/118589)
  - Plough (15–20 cm) vs rototiller (8–10 cm) made no significant difference to wheat yield.
  - Ploughing looked more suitable for fallow.
  - Rotation order: fallow–wheat best, winter lentil–wheat second, continuous wheat lowest.
  - The rototiller is suggested for quick seedbed preparation after lentil.
- After stubble in Central Anatolia, mibzer (drill) sowing is the standard. Broadcasting needs 3–5 kg/da more seed and causes uneven emergence and ripening — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)

### Inferences
- Site guidance for dry autumns:
  - prefer a drill that bands fertilizer away from the seed (about 7 cm side, 8 cm deep)
  - if only a same-band drill is available, broadcast and incorporate the fertilizer before sowing
  - DE is an option with a proper no-till drill, weed control and timely sowing; expect possible lower yields in the first transition years.

### Gaps
- I found no official tillage calendar for the fallow year (number and timing of passes) beyond the Haymana trial.
- I found no adoption figures for direct drilling in Central Anatolia.

## 4. Seed treatment, certified seed and varieties

### Takeaway
Seed treatment means treating cereal seed against smut and bunt (sürme, rastık) and the ground beetle Zabrus. Other recommendations: use certified seed or seed cleaned over a 2–2.2 mm sieve; for chickpea, use disease-free seed with 85–95% germination.

### Cited Findings
- Seed treatment: treating seed against fungal diseases such as **sürme (bunt) and rastık (smut)** and against soil-dwelling **Zabrus spp.** is "of great importance". Use **certified seed** of a high-genetic-value variety, or clean seed with a selektör before sowing — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- TİGEM screens seed over a **2–2.2 mm** sieve and treats it before calculating seeds/m² — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- TİGEM multiplies **34 bread wheat, 10 durum and 9 barley varieties** for farmers — [TİGEM-B4 [PAGE]](https://todab.org.tr/files/BUGDAY%20VE%20ARPA%20TARIMI/B%C3%B6l%C3%BCm-4.pdf)
- Trakya TAE also asks for seed treated against sürme and Zabrus — [TTAE [PAGE]](https://arastirma.tarimorman.gov.tr/ttae/Sayfalar/Detay.aspx?SayfaId=69)
- Grain legumes: preventive seed treatment before sowing is advised in the pea antraknoz section. For lentil collar rot (kökboğazı çürüklüğü), chemical control is by seed treatment — [GKGM-YB [PAGE]](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/GKGM/Belgeler/Uretici_Bilgi_Kosesi/Dokumanlar/yemeklik_baklagil.pdf)
- Chickpea varieties listed in GKGM-YB include Canıtez 87, Akçin, Gökçe, Aziziye, Uzunlu-99, Hisar, Azkan and Aksu — [GKGM-YB [PAGE]](https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/GKGM/Belgeler/Uretici_Bilgi_Kosesi/Dokumanlar/yemeklik_baklagil.pdf). The Konya trial used Karahan-99 wheat, Gökçe chickpea, Sultan-1 green lentil, Altınova vetch and Remzibey safflower — [Gültekin et al. 2017 [PAGE]](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/390551)

### Inferences
- The seed-quality inputs to the TKW formula (purity and germination) should come from the seed label or a simple home test, as TİGEM-B4 describes. That keeps the seed-rate output traceable as "user" or "official" values.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve current Bahri Dağdaş or TARM variety lists, or variety-specific seeds/m².
- I found no official list of active ingredients for seed treatment; that is out of scope and changes by registration.
