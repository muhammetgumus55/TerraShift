## 1. Ekim alanı ve üretimi en büyük ürünler (bölge ve il)

### Takeaway
Rainfed winter cereals dominate Central Anatolia: buğday (wheat) first, arpa (barley) second. Konya is Türkiye's largest producer of buğday, arpa, şeker pancarı, mısır, kuru fasulye and yonca. In the irrigated pockets, şeker pancarı, patates, mısır and kuru fasulye are concentrated:
- şeker pancarı: Konya, Eskişehir, Yozgat, Kayseri
- patates: Niğde, Konya, Nevşehir, Aksaray, Kayseri
- kuru fasulye: Konya, Niğde, Nevşehir, Karaman

Cool-season legumes (nohut, yeşil mercimek) are concentrated in Ankara, Yozgat, Kırşehir and Konya. Recent (2023–2025) per-province TÜİK tables for all 13 provinces were **not** retrieved. The richest verified province series is for Konya, 2016–2020.

## 2. Nadas (fallow) alanı: bölge ve il, değişim

### Takeaway
Central Anatolia holds most of Türkiye's fallow land: about 59% of 4.2 Mha around 2014. Fallow share varies a lot by province, from about 5% of farmland in Karaman to about 30–32% in Aksaray and Kırşehir (TÜİK 2020). Fallow is shrinking over time (Konya's sown area rose 2016→2020 because nadas fell), but no 2023–2025 il-level fallow table was retrieved.

## 3. Kuru ve sulu alan payı; hangi ürünler sulu, hangileri kuru

### Takeaway
Most Central Anatolian farmland is rainfed: annual rainfall is about 330–390 mm, and nadas is practised where rainfall is below about 500 mm. Irrigation is concentrated in Konya, Karaman, Aksaray and Niğde, using DSİ projects plus very large private and cooperative groundwater use. Şeker pancarı, patates, mısır, yonca, kuru fasulye and most ayçiçeği/soğan are irrigated. Buğday, arpa, nohut, mercimek, fiğ and aspir are predominantly rainfed.

## 4. Kuru İç Anadolu'da tipik ekim nöbetleri ve baklagil yaygınlığı

### Takeaway
The traditional rainfed system is **buğday–nadas** (2-year), often with continuous cereal (buğday–buğday or buğday–arpa) where rainfall allows. Legume-based rotations (buğday–nohut, buğday–mercimek, buğday/arpa–fiğ) are agronomically recommended and present in Ankara–Yozgat–Kırşehir–Konya, but less common than cereal–fallow. Oilseeds (aspir, ayçiçeği) are proposed as fallow replacements, yet farmers avoid them because they lower the following wheat yield. In irrigated land, şeker pancarı is limited to once every 4 years on the same field.

### Cited Findings
- [SNIPPET] In Orta Anadolu, nadas–buğday, baklagil–buğday and buğday–buğday rotations are all still widely practised (Ankara Üniv. PhD thesis). Nadas–buğday gave the highest wheat yield, followed by baklagil–buğday and then buğday–buğday. Buğday–buğday looked most economic but has disease and weed problems, so the thesis recommends baklagil–buğday. — search summary of [YÖK Açık Bilim thesis](https://acikbilim.yok.gov.tr/handle/20.500.12812/510095) / [related](https://acikbilim.yok.gov.tr/handle/20.500.12812/514487)
- [SNIPPET] Legumes suited to rotation with wheat in the region: kışlık mercimek, yazlık mercimek, nohut, macar fiği. — search summary ([dergipark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/390551))
- [SNIPPET] Most rainfed production in Orta Anadolu is wheat under conventional tillage. Wheat makes up about 70% of field-crop sown area (exact wording not verified). — search summary ([Tarım Makinaları Bilimi Dergisi](https://dergipark.org.tr/download/article-file/118979))
- [SNIPPET] In a 2004–2007 nohut–buğday rotation trial, direct seeding was more profitable for wheat, while conventional tillage was better for chickpea. — search summary ([TÜBİTAK Turk J Agric](https://aj.tubitak.gov.tr/agriculture/issues/tar-01-25-4/tar-25-4-6-0006-54.pdf))
- [PAGE] Aspir is the most suitable oilseed for buğday–nadas areas. Farmers avoid buğday–ayçiçeği and buğday–aspir because they reduce wheat yield. The paper proposes buğday–aspir–nadas or buğday–ayçiçeği–nadas (fallow kept just before wheat), which would cut the fallow ratio from 50% to 33%. Sugar beet is grown under "saha münavebesi", once every 4 years on the same field. — [Kaya et al. 2015](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/810019)
- [SNIPPET] Develi Ovası: first crops are generally arpa, buğday and fiğ, with silajlık mısır as a second crop, and silage maize area is growing. — [NTV Kayseri](https://www.ntv.com.tr/yerel-haberler/kayseri/kayseride-silajlik-misir-ciftcinin-yuzunu-gulduruyor,rB2YZvsLC0iwDGJPTJW-vw)

### Inferences
- Legume prevalence can be gauged indirectly. National chickpea and green-lentil production is concentrated in Ankara, Yozgat, Kırşehir and Konya, and KOP holds 47% of TR nohut and 78% of yeşil mercimek. So buğday–nohut and buğday–mercimek are most common on the northern and eastern rainfed plateau (Yozgat/Boğazlıyan, Kırşehir/Kaman, Ankara/Polatlı) rather than in Konya Ovası.
- Kaya et al. note that the guardrail concept for Arksus (the wheat yield penalty after sunflower or safflower) matches farmer behaviour.

### Gaps
- No survey-based percentage of farms or area under each rotation type was found. The figures are all qualitative or trial-based.

## 5. İlçe notları (13 lokasyon)

### Takeaway
Only fragmentary district-level information was found. Karapınar is the best documented: wind erosion, groundwater-based irrigation and obruk (sinkholes). The other districts have only qualitative notes.

### Inferences
- For Arksus's archetypes:

| Type | Districts | Main crops |
|---|---|---|
